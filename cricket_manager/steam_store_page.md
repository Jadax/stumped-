# Stumped! — Steam Store Draft

## Short description

Build a cricket dynasty in a deep, fast, data-driven management simulation.
Shape your squad, master every format, and make every ball count.

## About this game

Stumped! puts you in complete control of a professional cricket club. Recruit
and develop players, balance contracts and budgets, improve facilities, guide
academy prospects, and compete across a living domestic league and knockout
cup.

On match day, every delivery is simulated from player attributes, form,
conditions, tactics, and match pressure. Adjust aggression, rotate bowlers,
change the field, use DRS, and decide when to accelerate or survive. Play every
ball, complete an over at a time, or hand control to your staff.

From a tense T20 chase to a five-day Test turnaround, Stumped! combines the
speed of modern sports management with the statistical depth cricket deserves.

## Key features

- T10, T20, The Hundred (100 balls), ODI, and four-innings Test cricket
- Ball-by-ball tactical match management
- DRS, DLS, follow-ons, declarations, powerplays, and Super Overs
- Two-division league, promotion/relegation, cup, and friendlies
- Transfers, scouting, training, youth academy, facilities, and finances
- Hundreds of evolving players with form, potential, aging, and retirement
- Responsive dark interface designed for 720p through 4K
- Local saves with Steam Cloud preparation
- 20 planned Steam achievements
