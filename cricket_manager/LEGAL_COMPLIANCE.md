# Stumped! — Commercial Asset Compliance

- The game bundles only Inter under the SIL Open Font License and falls back to system fonts when necessary.
- Club crests, interface icons, pitch diagrams, and textures are generated from original geometric primitives at high resolution and downsampled for clean edges.
- Country identifiers use Unicode flag characters; West Indies uses a neutral regional symbol.
- No photographs, broadcast footage, real-player likenesses, or real club logos are included.
- Player headshots are deterministic, supersampled original Pygame illustrations generated
  from non-identifying player data; they do not reproduce real people.
- Inter is bundled unmodified under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Its copyright
  notice and complete license are included in `assets/fonts/OFL-Inter.txt`.
- New databases generate fictional player-name combinations from original pools.
- Audio is generated in-project and used only for optional match crowd effects.
- Background music is not loaded or played.
- Workshop imports must pass the same asset and naming checks before public distribution.

Real competition labels in the world-data catalog are descriptive configuration data requested for the simulation. A final trademark review remains a publisher responsibility before store submission.
