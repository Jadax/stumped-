# Stumped! Competitive Roadmap — v1.0 and Beyond

## Goal
Make Stumped! the best cricket management game on Steam. Better than Cricket
Captain 2026 and Cricket Management 26 in depth, UI/UX, and engagement.

## Phase 1: Professional Polish (v0.99.0 — immediate)
Make the game feel like a finished, professional product.

### 1.1 About Screen
- Game version, build date, credits
- ASTRAIVA (Pty) Ltd copyright
- Links to community/Discord/support
- Open-source attributions (fonts, flags)

### 1.2 Settings Page Overhaul
- Game Speed: Slow/Normal/Fast/Instant (already exists)
- Resolution: dropdown with common resolutions
- Fullscreen: toggle
- Volume: master/SFX/music sliders
- Auto-save: Off/Monthly/Weekly/Daily
- Commentary verbosity: Key Moments/Normal/Full
- UI Scale: 100%/125%/150%
- Difficulty: Easy/Normal/Hard/Custom
- Match simulation speed: Ball-by-ball/Over/Innings
- Reset tutorial button
- Credits button

### 1.3 Changelog/Version Screen
- In-game changelog viewer
- "What's New" overlay on first launch after update
- Version history browser

### 1.4 Match Day Polish
- Score bug redesign (team crests, large score, overs progress bar)
- Commentary cards grouped by over
- Tactic buttons with icons and visual grouping
- Ball-by-ball indicator (dots/wickets/fours/sixes)
- Match momentum graph (always visible)

### 1.5 Screen Transitions
- Smoother transitions (0.2s ease-in-out)
- Loading spinner for IPC calls
- Error toast notifications

## Phase 2: Scale & Content (v1.0.0)
Expand the world to competitive scope.

### 2.1 More Teams
- Expand from 24 to 50+ clubs
- Add 3rd division (12 more clubs = 36 total)
- Add regional teams for youth pathway
- Country-specific leagues (India, Australia, England)

### 2.2 More Players
- Expand from 600 to 1200+ players
- Better youth pipeline (academy graduates every season)
- Free agent pool
- Retired players archive (legends)

### 2.3 International Layer
- National team selection
- International fixtures (bilateral series)
- ICC tournaments (World Cup, T20 World Cup, Champions Trophy)
- World rankings

### 2.4 Enhanced Stats
- Career statistics tracking
- Season records
- All-time records
- Head-to-head records

## Phase 3: Engagement (v1.1.0)
Keep players coming back.

### 3.1 Steam Achievements
- 50+ achievements covering:
  - Career milestones (first win, first title, 100 matches)
  - Tactical achievements (hat-trick, double century, 10-wicket match)
  - Management achievements (undefeated season, promotion, treble)
  - Collection achievements (sign 50 players, reach max facilities)

### 3.2 Daily Challenges
- One daily scenario (e.g., "Chase 300 in 50 overs")
- Leaderboard for best scores
- Weekly challenges with bigger rewards

### 3.3 Career Milestones
- Manager career timeline
- Trophy cabinet
- Personal bests tracking
- Career statistics summary

## Phase 4: Polish & Depth (v1.2.0)
The details that make it best-in-class.

### 4.1 Player Development

- Mentoring system

- Form/confidence dynamics

### 4.2 Tactics Depth

### 4.3 Board & Finances

### 4.4 Staff System
