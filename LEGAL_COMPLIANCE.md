# Legal Compliance

Stumped! contains no licensed real-world cricket content. All player names,
faces, portraits, crests, kits, and audio are procedurally generated.

## Known exception (tracked)

The seed data still contains real-world club and venue names — see
`docs/CODE_AUDIT.md` item A ("Legal/compliance"). This is the top-priority
backlog item; remediation is a generated name pool for all clubs and grounds
plus a save migration. Do not add further real-world names, likenesses, or
trademarks anywhere in the codebase or assets.
